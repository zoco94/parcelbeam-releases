Stamped on every crate

Verified

Every file carries a sha256 checksum, checked on arrival. A corrupted transfer is rejected, never silently saved.

PIN lock

Require a PIN before anyone can send to you. Ten misses and the sender is locked out.

Share link

Serve files to any browser on your network — scan a QR with a phone, no app needed.

Encrypted

HTTPS transfers with certificate pinning against each device’s announced identity.

No telemetry

Zero analytics, zero tracking. Its only internet call is checking this page’s releases feed for updates.

Tray-ready

Lives in your tray or menu bar, receives while you work, and pops a notification when a parcel lands.

And an iPhone app of our own

📱

ParcelBeam for iPhone

On the App Store · Free

Any LocalSend app will talk to your desktop. The ParcelBeam app is built for it — and does more:

  • Share sheet — beam straight from Photos, Files or Safari.
  • Sends and receives, with an accept prompt and optional PIN.
  • Checksum-verified on the phone, not just the desktop.
  • A history that does something — preview any file, save photos to your library, or jump into the Files app.
  • Favourite devices so your Mac is preselected the moment it appears.
